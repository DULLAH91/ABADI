# Commercial & Investment Steering

## Mission
AI Media Hub must become a commercially defensible technology asset, not a demo.

## Commercial gates
Before adopting a dependency, model, dataset, node, skill, MCP server, or hosted API, record:
- License
- Commercial-use permission
- Attribution obligations
- Redistribution obligations
- Model/data restrictions
- Security posture
- Maintenance/activity
- Vendor lock-in
- Replacement strategy
- Estimated unit cost

## Revenue paths
Prioritize capabilities that can become:
1. Internal production efficiency
2. Productized service
3. SaaS feature
4. API
5. Enterprise/private deployment
6. Licensed workflow/IP

## Investment evidence
Track:
- Time saved per workflow
- Cost per generated artifact
- GPU utilization
- Throughput
- Failure rate
- Human review time
- Gross margin potential
- Reusable IP
- Customer validation
- Retention/usage when customers exist

## Moat
Defensibility should come from orchestration quality, evaluation, workflow intelligence, domain knowledge, integrations, operational data, reusable assets, and reliable execution—not from merely bundling open-source projects.

## Rule
If a technology increases complexity without improving product value, economics, reliability, or differentiation, reject it.
