# Commercialization

## Product ladder

### P0 — Internal Media Engine
Prove repeatable production workflows and unit economics.

### P1 — Productized workflows
Package high-value workflows such as:
- advertising creative factory
- product visual generation
- social content factory
- automated video editing
- documentary/storytelling pipeline

### P2 — Platform
Expose projects, workflows, jobs, assets, approvals, analytics, and APIs.

### P3 — Enterprise
Private deployment, SSO/RBAC, audit, isolated workers, policy controls, SLA-oriented operations.

## Revenue models
- Usage-based generation
- Workflow subscriptions
- API metering
- Enterprise licensing/private deployment
- Managed production service
- Premium workflow packs
- Integration/implementation services

## Investment thesis
The investment case should be based on:
- proprietary orchestration and evaluation
- reusable workflow IP
- measurable production economics
- multi-model/provider flexibility
- enterprise deployment capability
- customer evidence
- strong technical governance

## Do not claim
Do not claim proprietary rights over third-party open-source code, models, datasets, or hosted APIs. Clearly separate our original code/IP from third-party components.

## KPI set
- Jobs/day
- Successful completion rate
- Median job duration
- Cost/job
- Human minutes/job
- GPU hours/job
- Reuse rate of workflows
- Gross margin estimate
- API revenue
- Subscription revenue
- Enterprise pipeline
